    Who's That Baller?

    Each side hides an NBA great. Ask yes/no questions, flip down who doesn't fit, and name theirs first. A wrong guess loses.

    How to play

    1. Start by picking your secret player from the 24 (or tap Random pick). It stays face down; tap Show to check it.
    2. On your turn, ask one yes/no question. The answer comes from their secret player automatically.
    3. Flip down every card the answer rules out (tap a card, or turn on Auto-flip), then end your turn.
    4. Think you know it? Press Guess, tap the card and lock it in. A wrong guess loses the game.
    5. Talk mode: ask and answer out loud. The game keeps your board and secret player; flip your cards and tap End turn when you're done.
